Know Before You Go!

Want to explore the 7 seas of DFW, but aren't sure how certain places are in terms of sensory levels? Sealy and his friends are here to help so that you never have to experience the vast seas alone! Togeether, we make the seas a better and safer place for seals and explorers alike!

  • Light Levels

    This rating involves how intense the light fixtures are at this location. This includes both specific areas of intense light and overall lighting.

    Scale: 1 = little/no lighting to 10 = stepping into a light fixture display store.

  • Sound Levels

    This rating involves how loud a location is in terms of sources such as overhead radios, TVs, and music.

    Scale: 1 = little/no sound to 10 = rave party-esque levels of music.

  • Crowd Levels

    This rating involves how many people are usually at this location compared to the size of the space.

    Scale: 1 = sparse spread to 10 = shoulder-to-shoulder touch nearly all the time.

  • Smells

    This rating involves how intense the smells are, from foods, construction, or air fresheners.

    Scale: 1 = no distinct smells to 10 = intense scent immediately upon entering.

  • Temperature Control

    This rating involves how hot or cold a location is overall. Mention specific areas in your review comments.

    Scale: Press one of five buttons to describe how cold or hot it feels.

  • Overall Score

    Calculated by averaging the light, sound, crowd, and smell scores together.

    Scale: 1 = beware of intense sensory triggers to 10 = very sensory-friendly.
